    Golden rules

    • Sleep before a 5 AM call. After a 1 AM wrap, sleep in.

    • Use waiting time for 10-minute tasks only. Save deep work for easy days.
    • Keep your rest block. It protects your brain and your income.
    • Skip the workout on long shoot days. Sleep comes first.
